## Research Plan

### Pass 1: Landscape Survey
**Focus:** Map the entire space — categories, players, products.
**Questions:**
- What product categories exist in this space?
- Who are the established players and who are the startups?
- What's the user experience across categories — what works, what doesn't?
- Any recent funding rounds, acquisitions, or exits?
**Output:** `docs/research/01-landscape.md` — summary table + category breakdown

### Pass 2: User Voice
**Focus:** What are real users saying — reviews, forums, complaints, wishlists.
**Questions:**
- What do users praise about existing products?
- What are the most common complaints and unmet needs?
- Are there user segments with different needs that current products don't distinguish?
- What workarounds are people building for themselves?
**Output:** `docs/research/02-user-voice.md` — themes from user feedback with sources

### Pass 3: Competitive Deep-Dive
**Focus:** Detailed analysis of the top 5-8 competitors.
**Questions:**
- For each: product, pricing, features, reviews, distribution channel, apparent business model
- Where is each one strong and weak?
- What's their trajectory — growing, stagnant, declining?
- How defensible is each position — what's their moat?
**Output:** `docs/research/03-competitive-deep-dive.md` — comparison table + per-competitor analysis

### Pass 4: Market Structure
**Focus:** Market size, dynamics, and barriers.
**Questions:**
- What's the estimated market size? (TAM/SAM/SOM if you can find data)
- What are the business models — subscription, hardware margin, freemium, ads?
- What are the barriers to entry — hardware costs, regulatory approval, network effects?
- What's the distribution landscape — app stores, retail, DTC, B2B channels?
**Output:** `docs/research/04-market-structure.md`

### Pass 5: Positioning & Feasibility
**Focus:** Where does my concept sit and can I actually build it?
**Questions:**
- What's the strongest positioning for my concept relative to existing products?
- What's technically required for an MVP? What's commodity vs. novel?
- What could one person (with AI tooling) realistically build vs. what needs a team?
- What would a credible first version look like?
**Output:** `docs/research/05-positioning-feasibility.md`

### Pass 6: Synthesis & Decision
**Focus:** Integrate everything. Make a recommendation.
**Output:** `docs/decisions/market-assessment.md`

## Success Criteria

<!-- What does "enough research" look like? Examples: -->
- I can name the top 5 competitors and articulate why my concept is differentiated
- I have evidence-backed estimates of market size and growth
- I understand the technical requirements well enough to scope an MVP
- I have a clear go/no-go recommendation with supporting evidence
- I know what the biggest risks are and which ones I can mitigate

## Risks and Biases

<!-- Where are you likely to fool yourself? Examples: -->
- Confirmation bias: I already think this is a good idea. I need to actively search for reasons it isn't.
- Survivorship bias: I'll find the successful companies easily. I need to look for the ones that tried and failed.
- Builder bias: I might underestimate go-to-market difficulty because I'm focused on the product.
