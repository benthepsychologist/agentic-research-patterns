# Scientific Research

## What This Skill Does

Runs a structured evidence-oriented research sequence: field map, core evidence, competing models, methods/limitations, reading list, and synthesis.

## Use When

- Understanding a field
- Evaluating a body of evidence
- Preparing to speak intelligently with domain experts
- Distinguishing consensus from contested claims

## Deliverables

- Field map
- Core evidence memo
- Competing models comparison
- Methods / limitations note
- Reading list
- Synthesis with confidence levels
