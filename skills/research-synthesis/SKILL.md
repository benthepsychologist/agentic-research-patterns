# Research Synthesis

## What This Skill Does

Integrates multiple research passes into a coherent conclusion while preserving uncertainty and evidentiary grounding.

## Use When

- Multiple pass outputs already exist
- The user needs a decision memo, synthesis note, or recommendation
- The next step is interpretation, not more searching

## Deliverables

- Synthesis memo
- Decision document
- Updated state-of-knowledge review
