# Market Research

## What This Skill Does

Runs a structured market research sequence: landscape, user voice, competitors, market structure, positioning, and synthesis.

## Use When

- Evaluating a market or category
- Comparing competitors
- Pressure-testing a product concept against real user needs
- Preparing a positioning or go/no-go memo

## Deliverables

- Landscape map
- User voice memo
- Competitive analysis
- Positioning / feasibility memo
- Decision document
