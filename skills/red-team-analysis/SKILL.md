# Red-Team Analysis

## What This Skill Does

Attacks a position hard enough to expose weak assumptions, likely failure modes, and better alternatives before commitment.

## Use When

- Evaluating whether an idea survives contact with a smart critic
- Pressure-testing strategy or product concepts
- Preparing for objections from stakeholders, market, or reality

## Deliverables

- Counterarguments memo
- Assumption audit
- Failure mode register
- Alternatives memo
- Red-team verdict
