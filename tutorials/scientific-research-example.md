# Tutorial: Scientific Research from Field Map to Synthesis

This walkthrough demonstrates the scientific research pattern.

## Sequence

1. Initiate the project around a specific question
2. Build a field map
3. Review the core evidence
4. Compare competing models
5. Surface methods and limitations
6. Produce a reading list and final synthesis

## What to Notice

- Consensus and popularity are not the same thing
- Methods and limitations deserve their own pass
- Good synthesis preserves uncertainty rather than flattening it
