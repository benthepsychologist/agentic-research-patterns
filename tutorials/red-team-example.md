# Tutorial: Red-Team a Product or Thesis

This walkthrough demonstrates adversarial analysis.

## Sequence

1. State the position clearly
2. Build the strongest counterarguments
3. Audit assumptions
4. Map failure modes
5. Consider alternatives
6. Write the verdict: proceed, modify, abandon, or research more

## What to Notice

- The discomfort is the signal
- A red team pass should make the original owner flinch at least once
- The point is not to kill the idea automatically; it is to know what survives attack
