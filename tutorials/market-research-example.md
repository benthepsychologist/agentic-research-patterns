# Tutorial: Market Research from Zero to Positioning Memo

This walkthrough demonstrates the market research pattern end to end.

## Sequence

1. Run project initiation
2. Do a landscape pass
3. Gather user voice from reviews, forums, and complaints
4. Compare top competitors on consistent dimensions
5. Map market structure and barriers
6. Produce a positioning / feasibility memo
7. Write a final decision note

## What to Notice

- The user voice pass changes what counts as a real gap
- Competitor analysis is not just feature comparison; it is position comparison
- Positioning comes after evidence, not before it
