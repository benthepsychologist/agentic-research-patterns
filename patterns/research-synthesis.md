# Research Synthesis

## Use When

You already have multiple research passes and need to integrate them into a conclusion, recommendation, or decision document.

## Don't Use When

- There is not enough prior material to synthesize
- The user actually needs more evidence gathering, not a summary

## Inputs

- Initiation document
- Prior pass outputs
- Desired final deliverable type
- Decision threshold or audience

## Typical Pass Sequence

1. Review initiation goals and current evidence base
2. Identify conclusions supported by multiple sources/passes
3. Distinguish settled findings from uncertain ones
4. Compare findings to initial assumptions
5. Draft conclusions, risks, and next steps

## Outputs

- Synthesis memo
- Decision document
- Updated state-of-knowledge review

## Quality Checks

- Conclusions are explicitly grounded in prior passes
- Uncertainty is preserved, not hidden
- Risks and unresolved questions are surfaced
- The result is decision-useful, not just nicely written

## Failure Modes

- Premature certainty
- Pretty summary with no evidentiary spine
- Losing the distinction between evidence and inference
- Treating unresolved uncertainty as noise instead of signal
